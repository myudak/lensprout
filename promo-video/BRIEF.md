## Intent

A wordless motion-graphics promo for Lensprout, the Chrome screen recorder with automatic zoom
and a local editor. Used as the Chrome Web Store promo video (via YouTube), on the landing site
and on social media. Friendly and playful, in the brand's cream / coral / purple palette.

## Assets

- assets/fonts/ — Fredoka (headings) and Nunito (body), OFL, copied from @fontsource.
- assets/screens/ — the user's own editor and recorder screenshots (no macOS wallpaper, no faces).
- Mascot geometry inlined from `extension/src/components/brand/mascot-shapes.ts`.

## Customizations

- Animated logo intro: the mascot draws in, the eye blinks, the sprouts grow, then the wordmark.
- Feature beats: automatic zoom, editor timeline, 3D stage, webcam layouts, smart trim,
  MP4/GIF export, "private: nothing leaves your device".
- Outro with the logo and "Free on the Chrome Web Store".
- A 1080×1920 vertical cut (`vertical.html`) from the same scenes.
- Generated (synthesized, license-free) music bed and click sounds.
